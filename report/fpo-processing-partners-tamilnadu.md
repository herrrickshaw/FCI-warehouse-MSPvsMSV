# FPO partner identification — Tamil Nadu FPOs with existing processing capacity

*The [PPP model](../pitch/grain-value-loop-deck.html) needs a farmer-side partner that aggregates
paddy/oilseed and — ideally — already has some processing capacity to build on. This report screens
two official Tamil Nadu FPO directories to find those partners: which FPOs already own processing
machinery, and which sit on the right feedstock for each biorefinery stream. July 2026. Operational
annex to [ppp-rice-mills.md](ppp-rice-mills.md) and
[fiscal-rationalisation-procurement-vs-processing.md](fiscal-rationalisation-procurement-vs-processing.md).
Shortlist: [`data/tn_fpo_processing_partners.csv`](../data/tn_fpo_processing_partners.csv).*

## The two source directories

1. **List of FPOs in Tamil Nadu (2015)** — 58 FPOs, *with a machinery/warehouse/licence column set.*
   This is the useful one: it directly asks "do you have agri machinery / infrastructure?" and "which
   machinery?" — so it reveals *existing processing capacity*, not just names.
2. **FPOs under TNSFAC (XIII.13)** — 130 FPOs, district-wise, name + CEO contact only (no capacity
   columns). Useful for *feedstock matching* — many FPO names signal their crop (pulses, groundnut,
   coconut, maize, millet, delta paddy).

## The headline finding — and why it *is* the thesis

Screening the 2015 list's machinery column, **the overwhelming majority of FPOs report "NA" — no
processing machinery at all.** Only **three of 58** show any real processing/infrastructure capacity.
That is not a disappointment; **it is the investment thesis restated in primary data:** FPOs aggregate
and, at most, warehouse — the *value-added processing* (RBO extraction, dal milling, oilseed crush) is
the white space. The PPP model's logic — FPO aggregates the feedstock, the private/JV partner brings
the processing capital — is exactly what the capacity gap in this directory demands.

feedstock, a processing habit, and institutional credibility already in place.

## How to use this — a partnering sequence

1. **Anchor on a Tier-1 FPO for a pilot.** Nachalur (Karur) is the sharpest first call: it already owns
   processing machinery and grows the three key feedstocks — a real integrated-mill pilot could be
   structured around it under PMFME/PMKSY with the state RBO-offtake guarantee.
2. **Cluster Tier-2 FPOs by feedstock for scale.** For an RBO unit, the Thanjavur–Nagapattinam–Thiruvarur
   delta FPOs are the paddy-bran catchment; for dal, the Cuddalore/Thoothukudi pulse FPOs; for coconut
   oil, the Coimbatore/Thanjavur coconut FPOs.
3. **Bring the processing capital via the JV partner** — the capacity gap (55 of 58 FPOs report no
   machinery) is precisely what the private/JV leg of the PPP supplies; the FPO brings feedstock,
   aggregation and the farmer relationship, not the plant.

## Caveats

**The 2015 directory is ten years old** — FPO status, contacts, office-bearers and machinery will have
changed; every name here needs a fresh due-diligence call before it is treated as a live partner. The
machinery column is self-reported and sparse (many fields blank, not verified). The TNSFAC 130-FPO list
has no capacity data at all — Tier-2 fit is inferred from the FPO's crop/name and district, not from
confirmed processing assets. Contacts are reproduced from the **public government FPO directories** (SFAC/
TNSFAC published lists) for operational follow-up; treat them as leads to verify, not endorsements. This
is a screening/shortlisting exercise to seed partner outreach — **not** a recommendation to transact with
any specific entity, and not investment advice. A serious partner search would also pull the current
SFAC/NABARD FPO registry (post-2020 the 10,000-FPO scheme added many newer, better-equipped producer
companies not in these 2015-era lists).
