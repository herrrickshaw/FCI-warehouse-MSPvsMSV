# Public vs private in the trade of the main commodities — and how to shift it toward PDS & food processing

*Who actually moves India's key farm commodities across the border — the state/PSU/cooperative channel
or private trade — and how the split could be tilted so the same volumes feed PDS and domestic food
processing instead of leaking to private imports. July 2026. Data:
[`data/trade_public_private_split.csv`](../data/trade_public_private_split.csv). Companion to
[executive-summary.md](executive-summary.md) and [import-arm-evaluation.md](import-arm-evaluation.md).*

## The pattern: publicly *controlled*, privately *executed* — except oil

| Commodity | Flow | Private | Public channel | Public agency |
|---|---|---:|---:|---|
| Rice (basmati) | export $5.94 bn | ~98% | ~2% | NCEL G2G |
| Rice (non-basmati) | export $6.53 bn | ~90% | ~10% | NCEL / G2G; **bans+quotas = public control** |
| Wheat | export (banned) | — | — | 100% policy control |
| **Edible oil** | **import ₹1.68 L cr** | **~100%** | **~0%** | **NONE** (canalisation ended 1990s) |
| Pulses | import ~₹31,000 cr | ~80% | ~20% | NAFED/NCCF buffer + G2G |
| Cotton | domestic + export | ~70% | ~30% | **CCI** (MSP procurement) |
| Sugar | export (quota) | ~100% | — | private under govt MAEQ quota |

Two clear regimes emerge:

1. **Grain (rice/wheat): publicly controlled, privately executed.** The state owns the *policy* levers —
   export bans, quotas, G2G, and 100% of domestic *procurement* for PDS — but the physical export trade is
   private. The public role is control and procurement, not trading.
2. **Edible oil: the one commodity with *no* public channel at all.** ₹1.68 lakh cr of imports run entirely
   through private refiners/importers; the state canalisation (STC/NAFED, 1980s–90s) was dismantled. This is
   the single biggest gap — India imports its largest agri-commodity with zero public/cooperative arm.

Cotton and pulses sit in between: **CCI** already procures ~30% of cotton at MSP, and **NAFED/NCCF** run a
~20% public buffer in pulses — two existing public channels that can be *extended* into processing.

## How to alter the split for PDS & food-processing demand

The lever is not to nationalise trade — it is to **build a public/cooperative channel on the
*import-substitution* side**, so the volumes that today arrive as private imports are instead produced
domestically by public/cooperative processors feeding PDS and food-processing demand:

| Commodity | Shift | Mechanism |
|---|---|---|
| **Edible oil** | 100% private import → a public/coop **domestic-processing** channel | **HVOC** (revive) + **NAFED** oilseed procurement + **PACS/coop crush** + **CCI cottonseed** + **FCI rice-bran** → supply **PDS oil** domestically instead of imported palmolein ([executive-summary](executive-summary.md)) |
| **Pulses** | shrink ~₹31k cr private import | expand **NAFED/NCCF buffer** + **PACS dal-milling** → PDS pulses from domestic + buffer stock |
| **Rice / wheat** | already public on procurement | add **public/coop processing** — RBO from bran, atta/rice fortification (100% fortified rice already achieved), semolina/pasta — for the food-processing demand, not just raw PDS grain |
| **Cotton** | extend CCI's role | link **CCI-procured cottonseed** to public/coop oil crush — a domestic edible-oil stream parallel to FCI paddy→bran→RBO |
| **Sugar** | keep the model | the **ethanol-blending mandate** (regulated-private offtake) is the template — apply the same *offtake-mandate* logic to processed-grain oil/pulses for PDS |

**The principle:** the state already has the channels — **FCI** (grain), **CCI** (cotton), **NAFED/NCCF**
(pulses buffer), and a dormant **HVOC** (oil) — plus the **PACS/cooperative grid** now being built. Tilting
the split means *using these public/cooperative arms to process domestic feedstock into the PDS and
food-processing products India currently imports privately* — converting a private import bill into public/
cooperative domestic value, with PDS and fortification as the captive offtake.

## Caveats

Channel shares are **indicative** — India does not publish a clean public-vs-private split of trade by
commodity, so these are reasoned estimates from the known roles of CCI/NAFED/NCEL and the structure of each
market (basmati/oil = private; cotton/pulses = part-public), not audited percentages. Edible-oil "~0% public"
reflects the absence of a canalised import channel today, not zero government influence (duties, stock limits
remain). "How to alter" is a policy-design argument, not a forecast; each shift needs its own feasibility
(HVOC revival, NAFED capacity, PACS processing rollout). Research synthesis, not trade or investment advice.
